# StarPaper Privacy Policy / StarPaper 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-08-27

## English

StarPaper processes the videos you choose entirely on your Mac. It does not create an
account, connect to the internet, include advertising or third-party SDKs, or collect,
transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app stores its settings, the paths of selected videos, and read-only security-scoped
bookmarks locally in its own preferences or App Sandbox container. Bookmarks let the app
continue reading videos that you explicitly selected after the app restarts. StarPaper does
not modify or upload those videos.

macOS may write ordinary diagnostic information to the system log. StarPaper limits its own
diagnostic messages and does not send them to the developer.

Questions can be filed in the public repository's
[issue tracker](https://github.com/starsdaisuki/starpaper/issues).

## 简体中文

StarPaper 只在你的 Mac 本机处理你主动选择的视频。它不创建账号、不连接网络，
不包含广告或第三方 SDK，也不收集、传输、出售或分享个人数据。

应用会在本机自己的偏好设置或 App Sandbox 容器中保存设置、已选视频的路径，
以及只读的 security-scoped bookmark。Bookmark 只用于让应用重启后继续读取你明确
选过的视频；StarPaper 不会修改或上传这些视频。

macOS 可能会向系统日志写入普通诊断信息。StarPaper 会限制自己的诊断内容，
且不会把日志发送给开发者。

如有问题，可在公开仓库的
[Issues](https://github.com/starsdaisuki/starpaper/issues) 中联系。
